Built during OpenAI Build Week

Stitch started from one question: why does a five-line CI fix have to wait for a human to notice it first?

The problem

Anyone can paste a stack trace into a chat model and get a diagnosis. Nobody does that at 3am. The red X just sits there until someone opens their laptop — and the team loses hours to a fix that might be five lines.

The approach

Autonomous triggering, full-repo context, and branch-aware judgment — GPT-5.6 diagnoses, Codex writes the fix, a validation gate runs before anything opens a PR. A human stays in the loop exactly as much as they choose to.

Global behavior

Confidence engine: score blends log clarity, codebase familiarity, test coverage on the affected file, file count, and past fix success rate. 90–100% proceeds as configured; 70–89% downgrades one mode level automatically; 50–69% forces diagnose-only; below 50% is flagged complex and routed to a human, regardless of the configured mode.
